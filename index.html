About Me :)

I was born and raised in Nigeria, and the Maryland / DC area is home too. Right now I'm studying Computer Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Southern California.

What excites me most is where hardware meets software. To me, embedded systems are a lot like raising a child. A brand-new chip knows nothing about the world: it can't see, can't feel, and can't talk to anyone. You teach it slowly. Drivers give it senses, communication protocols give it a voice, and control logic gives it judgment, until one day it can act on its own.

I love building that up layer by layer, from the raw signals on a wire at layer 1 all the way up to the decisions at layer 7, and turning a blank piece of silicon into an autonomous system.
